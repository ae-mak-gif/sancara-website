Common Questions

Insurance Process Intelligence & Automation — FAQs

What is insurance process intelligence?

The disciplined mapping and analysis of how insurance work actually flows — people, documents, systems, decisions — so that improvement and automation are grounded in reality rather than assumption.

Which processes are good automation candidates?

Repetitive, rules-based, high-volume activities: document intake, data extraction, claims triage, workflow routing, status updates, report preparation, information requests and repetitive administration. Judgement-heavy work is supported, not replaced.

Does Sancara sell AI products?

Sancara is not a generic AI vendor. We combine insurance and risk expertise, process understanding and technology. Capabilities such as claims document intelligence, triage support and AI-assisted report preparation are areas of active development — they are described as emerging capabilities, not mature commercial products.

How do you avoid overpromising on AI?

Our methodology — Map, Identify, Transform, Automate, Verify — tests each automation or AI application against real workflow data and measured outcomes before scaling. If the evidence does not support it, we say so.

Common Questions

Climate-Related Risk Advisory — FAQs

What is a climate risk assessment?

A structured examination of how climate-related hazards could affect your assets, operations and organisation: hazard identification, exposure and vulnerability considerations, impact analysis and risk prioritisation — followed by adaptation and resilience options.

What does climate scenario and risk analysis add?

Scenarios explore how changing conditions — different climate pathways and time horizons — could alter future exposure, so decisions made today remain defensible under a range of plausible futures rather than a single forecast.

How does this relate to ESG and sustainability reporting?

Climate and sustainability risk analysis produces the information organisations need for ESG reporting, sustainability strategy and resilience planning — turning disclosure obligations into better internal decisions.
