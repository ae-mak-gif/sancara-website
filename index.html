About Sancara

Specialist thinking for a changing risk environment.

Sancara is a specialist risk firm focused on complex, emerging and interconnected risks affecting organisations, assets and operations.

We bring together experience across insurance and claims, engineering and technical analysis, process intelligence and automation, sustainability and climate risk.

Our work is grounded in a simple principle: organisations make better decisions when they can clearly understand what is happening, what it could mean, and how they can respond.

Why Sancara?

Risk is becoming harder to separate.

Physical losses can have operational consequences. Climate change can alter exposure to losses. Technology can transform insurance processes while introducing new risks. Sustainability and regulatory expectations can change how organisations measure and report risk.

Sancara exists at that intersection.

Our Approach

Five principles.

Evidence-led

We start with what the evidence shows — not assumptions.

Technically grounded

Claims, engineering, process and climate analysis done by people who understand the technical detail.

Practical

Recommendations organisations can actually implement.

Technology-enabled

We apply technology where it genuinely improves understanding, speed or quality.

Continuously learning

Risk environments change. So does our analysis.

02 — Insurance Process Intelligence & Automation

Transform risk processes.

Improve insurance workflows through process intelligence, automation and practical AI.

The Problem

Insurance processes contain work that doesn't always need to be manual.

Repetitive activities may include data entry, document handling, information extraction, workflow handoffs, manual triage, repetitive communication, fragmented information and slow reporting. We help identify where process intelligence, automation and practical AI genuinely add value.

What We Do

Three services.

Claims Process Automation

Identify and automate repetitive claims activities:

  • Document intake & data extraction
  • Claims triage & workflow routing
  • Status updates & report preparation
  • Information requests & repetitive admin

Insurance Workflow Automation

Connect people, processes and systems to reduce unnecessary manual work:

  • Process mapping & workflow redesign
  • Business process automation & integrations
  • Notifications, approvals & data movement

AI Solutions & Decision Intelligence

Explore practical applications of AI to insurance information, workflows and decisions:

  • Document intelligence & information extraction
  • AI-assisted review & knowledge retrieval
  • Decision support & AI-assisted workflows

Our proposition is not generic AI consultancy. It is insurance and risk expertise + process understanding + technology. Sancara's potential future intellectual property in this space — such as an AI Loss Adjuster Assistant, document intelligence, claims triage and AI-assisted report preparation — is in development and is not presented here as a mature commercial product.

Methodology

Map. Identify. Transform. Automate. Verify.

Map

Document the current process end-to-end.

Identify

Find bottlenecks, repetition and automation candidates.

Transform

Redesign the workflow around what adds value.

Automate

Implement practical automation and AI where it works.

Verify

Test, measure and refine against real outcomes.

03 — Sustainability & Climate Risk

Anticipate risk.

Helping organisations understand changing climate and sustainability risks and make better-informed resilience decisions.

The Problem

Climate and sustainability risks increasingly affect business decisions.

Organisations are grappling with physical climate exposure, extreme weather, water stress, heat, asset vulnerability, operational disruption, resilience, sustainability information and evolving reporting requirements. The question is what is changing, what could happen, and how to prepare.

What We Do

Three services.

Climate Risk Assessment

Identify and assess potential climate-related exposure affecting assets, operations and organisations — including physical climate-risk screening, asset exposure assessment, hazard identification, vulnerability considerations and risk prioritisation.

Climate Scenario & Risk Analysis

Explore how changing conditions could affect future exposure — scenario analysis, climate pathways, exposure analysis, sensitivity analysis and risk interpretation.

Sustainability, ESG & Resilience

Improve sustainability information, reporting and resilience planning — ESG reporting support, sustainability assessments, ESG maturity assessment, resilience planning, adaptation and sustainability strategy support.
